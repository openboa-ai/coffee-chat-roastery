# Standard Roastery Publication Contract

Every owner change is proposed on a branch and merged through a pull request.
Direct default-branch writes are outside the Standard Roastery flow. Required CI
validates the exact proposed tree, canonical index, declaration, contract pin,
and changed-path boundary before GitHub-native squash merge.

Every Standard Roastery fixes `CC-BY-4.0` for `roastery/beans/**`. Scope and
license are not user choices. The initialized declaration carries one
normalized, well-formed Unicode owner attribution and applies to every Bean
present at that commit. Attribution is plain text and cannot contain HTML angle
brackets. Origin URLs and the resources they identify remain outside this grant.

Before Init writes anything, the user must see and accept the exact declaration
and digest, the complete publication notice, and a rights-authority attestation.
Rejection, cancellation, invalid attribution, stale Preview, or failed
capability/ownership preflight permits no fork, branch, pull request, file, or
Registry write.

A passing publication check proves only that declared technical checks and an
explicit publisher attestation ran for the identified head. It does not prove
identity, ownership, legal sufficiency, truth, quality, safety, or endorsement.
